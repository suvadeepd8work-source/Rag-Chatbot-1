## Frequently Asked Questions

* ### Do I need a technical or coding background to join?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-remove-bca5f1fb-e4c1-4e78-a874-b74730361f09.svg)

  No. The bootcamp is beginner-friendly. You’ll learn practical prompt engineering, no-code/low-code workflows, and how to apply AI in real work.
* ### What experience level is ideal for this bootcamp?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  This bootcamp is for working professionals (0–10 years experience) in product, design, marketing, analytics, operations, or business roles who are looking to pick up AI skills to get better at their current role or to add AI credibility in their career switch journey . Final-year students are welcome
* ### Can students join the program?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Yes. Students—especially in their final year—can join and build portfolio projects that strengthen their resume and LinkedIn. This can help you stand out as your prepare for college placements.
* ### Are classes live or recorded?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  All classes are live and interactive. Class recordings are made available so you can revisit or catch up anytime.
* ### What if I miss a live class?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  You can watch the session recording, review shared resources, and post questions in the community to catch up on any missed class.
* ### How much time do I need per week?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Plan for 8-10 hours weekly for the duration of the bootcamp (10 weeks), including live sessions, hands-on projects, and mentor office hours.
* ### Which tools will I learn?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  You’ll work with leading LLMs and AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT/Claude/HuggingFace), AI workflow & automation (Zapier/Make/n8n, LangChain, LangGraph), voice platforms (eg., Deepgram, ElevenLabs), AI evaluation tools (eg.,Promptfoo, Trulens) and creative tools (eg.,Midjourney, Gemini Flash, Runway ML). Tooling evolves—expect up-to-date stacks.
* ### How are projects evaluated?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Every project receives mentor feedback on prompt quality, workflow design, reliability, and ethical considerations so you can iterate to portfolio quality.
* ### Will I work on real-world projects?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Yes. You’ll complete multiple hands-on projects plus a capstone that solves a practical use case.
* ### What makes this different from free YouTube tutorials?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Structured curriculum, live instruction from top industry experts, mentor office hours for guidance and review, role-specific workflows, and a portfolio. It’s applied, not passive viewing.
* ### Do you provide career support?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Yes. Portfolio reviews, guidance on showcasing AI projects, and LinkedIn positioning so employers can evaluate your practical skills.
* ### Will this help me get a job or promotion?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  You can expect bootcamp graduates to build demonstrable AI skills and projects that improve team productivity and credibility—often leading to role upgrades or new opportunities.
* ### Can I add the certificate to LinkedIn?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Yes. You’ll receive a verifiable Certificate of Accomplishment you can add to LinkedIn and your resume.
* ### What ROI can I expect from the bootcamp?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Learners commonly report saving 5–10 hours per week via AI workflows, improved output quality, and recognition as the AI champion on their teams.
* ### Is the program online and cohort-based?

  ![expand](https://assets.nextleap.app/images/accordian-add-db9d73dd-301d-4686-89e7-65fd05f7af21.svg)

  Yes. It’s a live, online, cohort-based experience with peers, instructors, and mentors supporting you throughout.
